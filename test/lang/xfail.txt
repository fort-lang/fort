# expected failure, a listed test that passes fails the run, so an entry
# leaves this list in the commit that makes its test pass. Entries are path
# prefixes relative to test/lang, so a directory covers everything under it.
#
# After T-021 the compiler produces a running program for everything but
# ownership beyond `del`, deferred statements and module-level data: the
# scalar half of the language with its control flow, its calls, its modules,
# its structs, its pointers, its enums and its switch, and now spans and
# strings with their headers, their index and span checks, their casts and
# their range loops, `new` and `del`, and `main(string@ args)`.

# deferred statements (T-023) and module-level data (T-024), plus the `fail`

# for the overwrite check of D17.11, which is T-022's, and not for spans, and
